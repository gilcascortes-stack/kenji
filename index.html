KENJI

A voice assistant for your Mac. Say what you want done; Kenji shows the terminal command and runs it only after you say “yes”.

Meet the creator 🏛️

How a conversation goes

Show me the five biggest files in my Downloads folder.
Here’s the command: ls -lS ~/Downloads | head -6
Shall I run it?
Yes.
Done! The results are next to me.

What Kenji does

🎙️

Listens on your Mac

Speech is transcribed locally with Whisper. Your voice never leaves the computer.

✅

Asks before acting

Every command is shown first. Deleting, moving, installing, sudo and network commands are flagged as risky.

🧪

Smart mode

Kenji becomes a curious scientist who researches your question on the web and cites his sources.

🏛️

Socratic mode

He questions your ideas one step at a time, then sums up what you discovered.

See Kenji in action

Kenji is a personal project, and its code is private. Gilberto demonstrates it, and how he tested it, in interviews. Ask Socrates about Gilberto to get in touch.

Safety

  • Kenji runs real shell commands. Read what he proposes before you say yes; silence or any “no” cancels.
  • His window server listens only on 127.0.0.1, your own Mac, and needs a secret token before it will start listening or act on anything.
  • Only the transcribed text is sent to Claude, never your audio.
